FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start the university application process?
Simply contact us via the form or phone. Our advisors will review your profile and guide you through choosing universities, preparing documents, and submitting applications.
What countries do you support?
We currently support students heading to the USA, UK, Canada, Germany, Turkey, France, Russia, and more. Visit our Countries page for full details.
Is the airport pickup service free?
The airport pickup service is available as part of our full package or as a standalone service. Contact us for current pricing and availability in your destination city.
How long does the admission process take?
It depends on the university and country, but typically ranges from 2 weeks to 3 months. We work to expedite the process and keep you updated every step of the way.
Do you help with student visas?
Yes! We provide comprehensive visa guidance including document preparation, embassy appointment scheduling, and interview tips.
